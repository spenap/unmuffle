    3. **Test 1 - Spanish voice message:** Open WhatsApp, find a Spanish voice message, tap Share, select TransChat. Expected: progress spinner appears for 15-60s, then result screen shows Spanish transcript text with Copy and Share buttons.

    6. **Test 4 - Back during inference:** Share another voice message. While the spinner is showing, press Back. Expected: returns to the previous app immediately (no ANR, no crash).
    7. **Test 5 - No ANR:** During inference (spinner showing), tap around the screen. Expected: no "App Not Responding" dialog -- the UI remains responsive.
    8. **Test 6 - Silent/noise audio (optional):** If available, share a very short or silent audio clip. Expected: "No speech detected" message instead of hallucinated text.
